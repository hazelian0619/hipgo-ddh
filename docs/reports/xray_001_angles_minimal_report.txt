- Preoperative detailed assessment and comprehensive imaging
- Close follow-up (every 1-2 months)

**Prognosis Assessment:**
The prognosis is complex. Severe DDH may require multidisciplinary treatment and carries a risk of long-term complications, including early-onset osteoarthritis. Timely intervention is crucial for improving outcomes.
